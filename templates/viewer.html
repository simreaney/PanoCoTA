How to use the viewer

  • Drag the panorama to look around and explore the scene.
  • Use your mouse wheel or trackpad scroll to zoom in and out.
  • Clicking (or tapping on a mobile device) a "Scene link" hotspot will move you to another scene. They must also be used to return to a previous scene.
  • Hovering over (or tapping on a mobile device) other hotspot types reveals additional content.
