Navigation

  • Drag the panorama to look around and explore the scene.
  • Use your mouse wheel or trackpad scroll to zoom in and out.
  • Clicking or tapping a scene-link hotspot moves you to another scene and can also take you back to a previous one.
  • Hovering over other hotspots on a desktop, or tapping them on a touch screen, reveals additional content.
